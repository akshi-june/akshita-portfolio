01Transformation story

How I turn AI into business value

Seven stages, from the first conversation about a problem to the operating model that keeps the result. Select a stage to read how I run it.

  1. Understand workflows, friction, user needs, and measurable business outcomes.

  2. Identify where AI can automate, augment, or reimagine work and prioritize opportunities based on value, feasibility, risk, and readiness.

  3. Translate domain knowledge, workflows, business rules, edge cases, and success criteria into requirements for reliable AI solutions.

  4. Test AI capabilities with real users and workflows before scaling.

  5. Evaluate adoption, quality, time saved, productivity, user feedback, and business value.

  6. Move successful capabilities from experimentation into enterprise workflows.

  7. Establish ownership, controls, operating rhythms, training, and change strategies required for sustainable adoption.
